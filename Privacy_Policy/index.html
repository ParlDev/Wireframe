Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026

Please read this Privacy Policy (the "Policy") carefully and in its entirety. By continuing to look at this website (the "Website"), You acknowledge that You have read, understood, and been mildly surprised by the contents herein.

1. Definitions

1.1. "Operator" means Parley Murdock, a college student.
1.2. "You" means any person, or any entity that has somehow acquired a web browser.
1.3. "Information" means any fact, datum, detail, particular, or morsel pertaining to You, whether or not You consider it interesting.

2. Statement of Non-Collection

The Operator does not collect Information. The Operator is unable to collect Information. The Operator has considered collecting Information, has been advised against it, and has since moved on.

3. Inability to Collect

The Operator's inability to collect Information arises from a combination of factors, including but not limited to: a lack of infrastructure, a lack of ambition, and a sincere uncertainty as to what he would do with it. Should the Operator ever acquire such Information, he would not know where to put it.

4. Cookies

This Website does not use cookies. This includes, without limitation, chocolate chip, oatmeal raisin, and snickerdoodle.

4.1. Sole Exception. The Website stores two (2) pieces of data, namely Your choice between light mode and dark mode and the last given WWR standing. These are saved on Your own device, in a place called "localStorage," and is never transmitted to the Operator or to any other party. The Operator has no knowledge of whether You prefer light or dark mode and respects You equally either way.

5. Third Parties

The Operator does not share Information with third parties, as the Operator does not possess Information, and the Operator does not have third parties. The images in the carousel are not watching You. They are merely images.

6. Data Retention

Information that has not been collected cannot be retained. The retention period for Your Information is therefore zero (0) days, which is also the maximum permitted period.

7. Your Rights

7.1. Right of Access. You may request a copy of the Information the Operator holds about You. The Operator will provide a complete copy promptly. It will be blank.
7.2. Right to Erasure. You may request that the Operator delete all Information held about You. This request has already been fulfilled in advance.
7.3. Right to Object. You may object to the processing of Your Information. The Operator will note Your objection and will not process anything.

8. Children

The Website is not directed at children, and the Operator does not knowingly collect Information from children. The Operator does not knowingly collect Information from adults, pets, or ghosts either.

9. Hosting

The Website is hosted on a Raspberry Pi. The Raspberry Pi has been instructed not to collect Information. The Raspberry Pi was not consulted and has not provided comment.

10. Changes to This Policy

The Operator may amend this Policy at any time. You will not be notified of such changes, because the Operator does not have Your email address, which is a direct consequence of Section 2.

11. Contact

Questions regarding this Policy may be directed to the Operator through parl.tv. The Operator cannot contact You first, as he does not know who You are. This is, again, intentional.

This concludes the Privacy Policy. Thank you for Your time, which the Operator has not recorded.
